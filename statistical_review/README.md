# Independent statistical review (16 November 2026)

On 16 November 2026 an independent professional statistician, paid by the author for the review and who asked to remain anonymous, reviewed the equivalence tests of H1–H3, the Holm correction, the clustering checks and the simulations against the scripts, protocols and result files of this package.

This was a review of the distributed material, not a new run of the pipeline. The full re-runs of the package are documented in `reproduction/`.

| File | Content |
|---|---|
| `report_2026-11-16_it.md` | The report as received, in Italian, unchanged |
| `report_2026-11-16_en.md` | English translation by the author (AI-assisted); tables and numbers unchanged |

The H3 differences in the report are in hours of MAE, as in `preregistration/` result files (`dMAE`); the margin is 2% of the MAE of the lines-only model. The paper summarises the review under Declarations and reproduces it in Online Appendix I.
